Reading the figures

Speedup is vendor time divided by BatchLAS time. Anything above 1× means BatchLAS is faster. The BatchLAS arm is pinned to native. A cell whose native pin fell back to a vendor route is dropped, not plotted. An open marker means the BatchLAS arm still calls a vendor sub-op, such as cuBLAS gemm. The n-figures use the largest batch at which both arms were measured.
